The Story Behind rmLv

Real feelings, old memories, and words that finally found their way into songs.

Hi, I'm rmLv. Welcome to my world.

I'm someone who turns feelings, memories, and experiences into songs. My musical journey began with lyrics—words I wrote because there were things in my heart I didn't know how to say out loud.

For years, I kept writing song after song, dreaming of hearing those words become real music. I couldn't sing them myself, but I never stopped carrying that dream.

A feeling that began in 2010

Some stories stay with you long enough to become music.

In 2010, I met someone who would become the inspiration behind so much of my music. I'll call her L.

From that day on, I carried feelings I didn't always know how to express. I started writing lyrics, song after song, putting into words the things I couldn't say out loud. I had the stories and the emotions, but I didn't know how to turn them into songs because I couldn't sing.

Then AI tools gave me a way to bring that dream to life. I write my own lyrics, drawing from my experiences and the feelings I've carried for years, and use creative tools to help turn those words into music and videos.

Many of my songs are about L. I fell deeply in love with her, even knowing she might never feel the same way. I can't simply switch those feelings off, so I turn them into lyrics, melodies, and songs that say what my heart has struggled to express.

I don't make music because I have everything figured out. I make it because sometimes, writing is the only way I know how to let my feelings breathe.
